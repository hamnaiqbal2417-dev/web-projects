How it works

Everything a bank does, minus everything that wastes your afternoon.

Three things run every independent business: money coming in, money going out, and money set aside for later. Northbound is built around exactly those three, and nothing else.

In

Get paid without the wait

Share a Northbound link on any invoice. Clients pay by card or bank transfer, and funds clear same-day — not the standard three-to-five business days most banks quote by default.

Out

Pay contractors and bills on autopilot

Set recurring payroll or vendor payments once. Northbound holds the funds until the due date and sends them automatically, so nothing slips because you were heads-down on client work.

Aside

A tax reserve that fills itself

Tell Northbound your estimated tax rate and it sets money aside from every deposit automatically, into a separate sub-account you can see but can't accidentally spend.
