About us

A school that sees every child as an individual.

At Al-Falah, we believe education isn't confined to textbooks. Every class is kept small so teachers can give each student real attention. Science, the arts, and sports all carry equal weight in our curriculum.

Our goal isn't just good grades — it's raising citizens who can face the challenges ahead with confidence and responsibility.

"Education doesn't just teach you to answer — it teaches you to ask."
— The school's guiding principle, since 1994
Academics

Three stages, one purpose.

From nursery to matric, the curriculum at every stage is shaped around the age and ability of the students in it.

01 — Junior

Nursery to grade 5

A focus on foundational reading, math, and social skills, taught through play-based learning.

02 — Middle

Grades 6 to 8

Deeper work in science, computing, and languages, alongside debate and presentation skills.

03 — Matric

Grades 9 to 10

Board exam preparation, science and commerce streams, and career counselling sessions.

Admissions

How to start a new session.

The admissions process is simple and clear — it takes four easy steps to complete.

Get the form

Pick up an admission form from the school office or download it from the website.

Submit documents

Bring the B-Form, the previous class's result card, and two photographs to the office.

Entry test or interview

A short assessment appropriate to the grade, or a conversation with the parents.

Confirm admission

Once results are out, the seat is confirmed after the fee is paid.
