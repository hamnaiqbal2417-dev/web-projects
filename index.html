Weeknight · One Pan · Serves 4

Chicken
Karahi.

Tomato and green chili cooked hard and fast in a wok-shaped karahi until they collapse into a glossy, ginger-sharp sauce clinging to bone-in chicken.

15mPrep
35mCook
4Servings
Heat · 3/4
Ingredients

The base

  • 1 kgchicken, bone-in, cut into 8 pieces
  • 5ripe tomatoes, roughly chopped
  • 1 cupghee or cooking oil
  • 6–8green chilies, slit

Aromatics

  • 2 tbspginger, julienned
  • 1 tbspgarlic paste
  • 1 tspcumin seeds

Spice

  • 1 tspred chili powder
  • 1 tspcoriander powder
  • 1 tspsalt, to taste
  • 1 tspgaram masala

To finish

  • 2 tbspfresh coriander, chopped
  • 2lemon wedges
Method
01

Sear the chicken

Heat the ghee in a karahi or heavy wok over high heat. Add the chicken and sear until the pieces lose their pink color and pick up a light golden edge.

6–8 min
02

Build the base

Add ginger, garlic paste, and cumin seeds. Stir until fragrant, then tip in the tomatoes and green chilies. Keep the heat high — the tomatoes should break down fast, not stew slowly.

10–12 min
03

Season and reduce

Stir in the chili powder, coriander powder, and salt. Keep cooking, stirring often, until the oil separates and floats visibly at the edges of the pan — this is the signal the sauce is ready.

12–15 min
04

Finish

Sprinkle garam masala over the top, then scatter fresh coriander. Squeeze lemon over the whole pan just before serving with warm naan or steamed rice.

2 min
Note — if the oil hasn't separated by minute 15, the tomatoes were watery. Keep the heat high and uncovered; don't add water to rush it.
